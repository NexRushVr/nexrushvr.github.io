How to have smooth tracking?
  • Properly cool your PC. I was thermal throttling my system for a year, trying to chase every gremlin and reflective surface in my room. The reality was that when my PC was thermal throttling, my right hand and hip would float off into space. Now with a proper cooling setup I'm not seeing those issues at all.
